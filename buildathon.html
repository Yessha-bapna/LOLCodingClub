PROBLEM STATEMENT / 01

Construction Site Intelligence Platform

Construction teams generate site photographs, inspection reports, safety incidents, worker observations, material records and progress updates every day. Build an intelligent platform that organizes and analyzes this scattered information, helping project managers understand site activity, track progress, identify safety issues, detect recurring problems and determine which areas require attention using AI/ML and Generative AI.

PROBLEM STATEMENT / 02

Smart Municipal Complaint & Resolution Intelligence Platform

Municipal authorities receive complaints about potholes, garbage, drainage, water leakage, damaged streetlights and other civic issues through many different channels. Build an intelligent platform that helps citizens and municipal teams report, classify, prioritize, assign, track, resolve and analyze these complaints using AI/ML, geospatial analysis and Generative AI.

Read Full Problem Statement Open document ↗
PROBLEM STATEMENT / 03

Intelligent Food Safety Inspection & Risk Management Platform

Food-safety authorities inspect restaurants, hotels, cafeterias, food trucks and institutional kitchens, but growing workloads make it difficult to identify which establishments need attention first. Build an intelligent platform for establishments, inspections, violations, corrective actions and compliance, using analytics and AI/ML to identify high-risk cases and support better inspection decisions.
